---
name: German content preference
description: Product-owner rationale behind the main site's German language preference.
---

Product-owner decision: prefer the German version when the site offers one.

**Why:** The user explains that an entry URL may serve English while the questions, personas, and report are German.

**How to apply:** Preserve this product rationale when changing main-site language selection or introducing analysis-language settings; do not treat the German preference as an accidental default.
